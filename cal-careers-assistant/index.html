Privacy Policy — CalCareers Assistant

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Introduction

CalCareers Assistant is a browser extension (Chrome and Firefox) that helps job seekers manage their applications on calcareers.ca.gov. It is an unofficial, independent helper tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the State of California, CalHR, or CalCareers.

This policy describes what data the extension stores on your device, and the one case where data is sent elsewhere.

Data Stored Locally

The extension stores the following data in the browser's built-in local storage, organized into separate categories:

  • Settings — your extension preferences: whether the extension is enabled, whether hidden vacancies are shown, your title stop-list, per-section visibility of the job info panel, your plan tier, your theme (light/dark/auto), and the date of the last automatic cleanup.
  • Positions — vacancies you have individually hidden.
  • Departments — departments you have seen, with any color you assigned and whether you chose to show or hide them.
  • Colors — the palette of colors you have added for labeling departments.
  • Exams — assessment records and assessment applications you have manually entered.
  • Submitted — applications you have manually logged as submitted.
  • Watchlist — vacancies you have marked as "want to apply".

This data stays on your device. It is never synced to a server and never shared with any third party, except when you choose to use Report a Bug (see below).

Report a Bug (Opt-In)

The dashboard includes an optional "Report a Bug" feature, reachable via the sidebar. If you open it and click "Send", your report includes:

  • The bug description text you typed.
  • Your email address, if you chose to enter one — the field is optional.
  • Technical diagnostics: the extension's version number, the browser name ("chrome" or "firefox"), and your operating system name (e.g. "Windows", "macOS") — without a precise version number.
  • Counts of your saved data: how many entries exist in each category listed above (for example, the number of colors, departments, or positions you've saved), plus a few settings values (such as your theme and plan tier). Only counts and settings values are included — no titles, department names, job IDs, or other content from your stored data.

Nothing is sent unless you click "Send". No analytics or tracking service is used in this release.

Third-Party Service Providers

Report a Bug submissions go to a Google Form, which stores responses in a Google Sheet. Once your report reaches Google's systems, its further handling is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by us.

Data Retention

  • Local data (settings, positions, departments, colors, exams, submitted, watchlist) stays on your device until you clear it yourself, remove individual entries through the dashboard, or uninstall the extension.
  • Bug report data stored in the Google Sheet is retained until manually deleted by the developer. There is currently no automatic deletion schedule for this data.

Your Privacy Rights, Including California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

Because almost all data managed by this extension never leaves your device, you already have direct control over it: you can view, export, edit, or delete it at any time through the dashboard, or remove it entirely by uninstalling the extension. No request to us is needed for that data.

For the limited data you submit through the opt-in Report a Bug feature (see above), you have the right to know what was submitted and to request its deletion from the Google Sheet. To make such a request, contact stingwellcom@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the Effective date at the top of this document will be updated. The link to this policy referenced in the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons listings always points to the current version at a permanent URL — older versions are not published as separate files.

Contact
